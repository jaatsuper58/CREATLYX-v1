## Full description
ChattlyX is a privacy-first messenger built around one idea: your
conversations belong to you, and to the person you're talking to — nobody
else.

**Messaging that stays yours**
- End-to-end encrypted one-to-one and group chats.
- Encrypted photos, videos, voice notes and files.
- Delivery and read receipts, typing indicators — all encrypted too.

**Calls without the eavesdropping**
- Encrypted audio and video calls with an on-screen padlock for every peer.

**Designed for real life**
- Works on phones from 3 GB RAM upward; a light app that respects your data.
- 12 languages at launch, more to come.
- No ads. No trackers. No selling your data.

ChattlyX is in beta. We move fast, keep a changelog, and listen — join the
community channel from Settings → Help.
